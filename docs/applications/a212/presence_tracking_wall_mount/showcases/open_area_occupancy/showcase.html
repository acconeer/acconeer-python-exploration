Metrics and definitions

MetricDefinitionBasisResult
Presence detection The presence of one or more people in the area is detected.
Detection rate Share of the time with people present in which presence was detected (recall). The first 1.1 s after someone enters the empty area (the mean detection latency) is not scored. 7 min 99.9%
False detection rate Share of the time with the area empty in which presence was detected, scored on recordings of the empty room. 10 h 0.00%
Detection latency Time from a person entering the empty area until presence is detected.
Mean over entries.		 5 entries 1.1 s
People counting The number of people in the area is counted.
Counting accuracy Share of the time with people in the area in which the count matches the ground truth. The first 1.1 s after each entry (the mean detection latency) and 10.0 s after each exit are not scored. 6 min 85%
Counting accuracy (±1) Like counting accuracy, but a count within one person of the ground truth is also right; counting 0 while people are present never is. 6 min 99.8%
Mean error Mean of the counted minus the true number of people over the same time as counting accuracy: positive is overcounting, negative undercounting. 6 min +0.12 people

How we tested

Setup

RoomOpen lunch area with round tables and bench seating, opening onto the office and the kitchen: people come and go beyond the detection area. A desk fan runs on a table at (−0.95, 1.95) m.
HardwareXE212 (A2 evaluation kit)
Ground truthPeople in the detection area, annotated per radar frame from a synchronised camera video.
SoftwarePresence Tracking Wall Mount reference application in Exploration Tool v8.0.0. Default configuration (Exploration Tool preset) with the below modifications:
Sensor installation2 m above the floor, tilted 20° down.
Detection area−3 to 3 m in x, 0.1 to 6 m in y; −80° to 80° azimuth, −30° to 30° elevation.
We recommend a margin of about 10 cm between the detection area and the walls for best performance.
Exclusion zonesx = −0.95 m, y = 1.95 m, radius 0.5 m (desk fan position, detections inside this circle are ignored).
Place an exclusion zone wherever a stationary object that moves is detected as a person, with a radius large enough to cover all of its detections.
