###################
Open area occupancy
###################
A single wall-mounted A212 radar sensor detects, counts and tracks the people in a defined part of an open space – a group standing and talking, and a passer-by walking through. This page shows how the :ref:`Presence Tracking Wall Mount <a212_presence_tracking_wall_mount>` application was tested in this scenario, and the results.
