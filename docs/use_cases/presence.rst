Presence Detection
##################

Acconeer offers Presence Detection both for A121 and A212. Presence detection
refers to the ability to detect the presence or absence of people or moving
objects within a certain area. This capability is essential in various
applications, including smart home automation, security systems, energy
management, and occupancy monitoring. Presence detection systems detect
movement or changes in the environment, enabling responsive actions such as
turning on lights or other devices, triggering alarms, or adjusting climate
controls. The multi-antenna device support a wide range off use cases such as:

- Use Interaction with various devices, such as keyboards, monitors,
  thermostats, vending machines, doorbells, beds etc.
- Location and status of the user, such as TV, HVAC, Bathrooms, light
  fixtures, fall detection, conference system, smart homes and more.
- Intention and flow, such as entrance gates, building entrances elevators
  or in store experiences.

Acconeer Offering
=================

Acconeer's sensors use state-of-the-art :term:`PCR` technology to deliver accurate
and reliable presence detection for a wide range of use cases. To address these
use cases we have created ready-to-use Presence :term:`Detectors <Detector>`.

- A121 :doc:`Presence Detector</detectors/a121/presence_detector>`: This
  Detector identifies fast and slow movements within a specified range. Very
  good for use cases that only requires that human presence is identified.
- A212 Presence Application: This detector identifies fast and slow movement
  but also outputs the angle to the detection as well as tracking multiple
  persons within the specified range and counting the number of detections.
