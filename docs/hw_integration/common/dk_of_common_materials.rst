Dielectric constant of common materials
---------------------------------------

.. table:: Relative permittivity of common materials [#Boriskin2017]_
    :align: center
    :widths: auto

    ==================================== =====================================
    Material                             :math:`\varepsilon_r` at 60 GHz
    ==================================== =====================================
    Acrylic glass                        2.5
    Alumina                              9.3
    Fused Quartz                         3.8
    MACOR                                5.5
    PEEK                                 3.12 [#Naftaly2018]_
    PMMA                                 2.6  [#Gregory2021]_
    Polycarbonate (PC)                   2.8  [#Gregory2021]_ [#Hasegawa2017]_
    Polyethylene (PE)                    2.3  [#Hasegawa2017]_ [#Lamb]_ [#Salski]_ [#Afsar]_
    Polypropylene (PP)                   2.2  [#Hasegawa2017]_ [#Afsar]_
    Polystyrene (Rexolite)               2.5
    PTFE (Teflon)                        2.05 [#Hasegawa2017]_
    ==================================== =====================================
